I am a Ph.D. student in a joint doctoral program between Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII) and Fudan University. I am advised by Xudong Pan and Min Yang, and co-advised by Xipeng Qiu.

I work on AI safety and autonomous agents: how to evaluate what capable systems can do, observe how they reason, and build safeguards that hold up outside a static benchmark.

My current research includes executable environments for frontier-risk evaluation, reasoning-trace safety, and self-evolving cybersecurity agents.
