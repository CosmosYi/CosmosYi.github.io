Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in a joint doctoral program between Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII) and Fudan University, advised by Asst. Prof. Xudong Pan and Prof. Min Yang, and co-advised by Prof. Xipeng Qiu.

My research focuses on safety, alignment, and capability development for frontier AI systems. I work on scalable oversight, automated risk evaluation, and recursive self-improvement in agentic systems, while also exploring embodied intelligence.
