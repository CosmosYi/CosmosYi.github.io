Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in a joint doctoral program between Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII) and Fudan University. I am advised by Asst. Prof. Xudong Pan and Prof. Min Yang, and co-advised by Prof. Xipeng Qiu.

My research focuses on safety, alignment, and capability development for frontier AI systems. I work on scalable oversight and automated risk evaluation for advanced AI systems, as well as self-improving agent systems that learn from experience.
